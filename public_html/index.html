Before you get in touch

What helps us understand your event.

You don’t need every answer. Share what you know and we’ll talk through the rest.

  • Date and timing

    A preferred date, or a rough month if it is still open.

  • Venue or location

    Where the event will take place, or the area you have in mind.

  • Guests and audience

    Estimated guest numbers and who will be attending.

  • Budget range

    A budget range helps shape realistic options.

  • Format and programme

    Serving style, schedule, speakers or exhibitor needs.

  • Dietary requirements

    Any dietary needs to plan around for catering.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a confirmed date or budget to enquire?

No. Date and budget can stay unspecified. Share what you have in mind and we can discuss the rest.

What if I’m not sure which service I need?

Choose “Not Sure Yet” on the enquiry form and describe your plans. We’ll talk through which support fits.

Can I combine services for one event?

Yes, you can discuss more than one area, for example a conference with catering. Mention everything you need in your event details.

Do you publish packages or prices?

Not on the website. The scope, availability and price of any service are agreed for your event.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

Your enquiry goes straight to our inbox with a reference number, and we’ll reply to the email address you provide.
