About us

About The House of Adam Events

The House of Adam Events brings together event catering, advertising, exhibition and fair organisation, and conference organisation. Our starting point is your brief: the occasion, the people attending and what you want the event to achieve.

Our approach

A conversation around your requirements.

Every event has its own priorities. Tell us about your venue, timing, audience and budget so we can discuss the support you are looking for. Whether your enquiry concerns catering or an organised business event, a clear brief helps shape the next steps.

Because we work across four connected areas, you can bring us one part of an event or several. A product launch may need advertising and catering; a trade fair may need exhibition planning and a conference programme. We look at the whole picture with you.

How we work

What you can expect when you plan with us.

Simple principles that guide every conversation, whatever the size or type of event.

  • Your brief comes first

    We start from what you want the event to achieve, not from a fixed package.

  • Clear conversations

    We talk through venue, timing, audience and budget in plain terms, so decisions are easy to make.

  • Scope agreed upfront

    Scope, availability and price are agreed for your event before anything goes ahead.

  • Services that combine

    One enquiry can cover several areas, such as a conference with catering or an exhibition with advertising.
